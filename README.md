# Hi, I'm Shreyas

I'm a Computer Engineering grad student at USC who likes AI that has to survive the
real world. Most of my work sits where machine learning meets hardware: models squeezed
onto small devices, sensors that need to agree with each other, and software that keeps
going when something upstream breaks.

Before grad school I worked on warehouse safety at RAMS, getting person detection to run
on tiny camera chips fast enough to stop a forklift in time. These days I'm spending
more time on LLM systems and agents.

## What I'm building

- **[Switchboard](https://github.com/shreyasnimbalkar/switchboard-llm-gateway)**: a
  gateway that sits in front of several LLM providers and keeps requests flowing through
  outages and rate limits, while tracking what everything costs.
- **[Job Fit Agent](https://github.com/shreyasnimbalkar/-job-fit-agent)**: a team of
  small agents that compares a resume with a job posting and tailors it without
  inventing anything.
- **[Smart Scrub](https://github.com/shreyasnimbalkar/Smart-scrub--automated-data-cleaning)**:
  ML-assisted data cleaning, which I wrote up in a published research paper.
